ZIM Creator

Turn websites, archive.org books, ebooks, documents and pictures into ZIM files for Kiwix.

1. What to convert

    Drag files, or links from your browser, anywhere onto this window — or
    Which files work?
    • Ebooks: EPUB, MOBI, AZW, AZW3, FB2
    • Documents: Word (DOCX), OpenDocument (ODT), RTF, PowerPoint (PPTX), Excel (XLSX), CSV, Markdown, plain text, HTML, Jupyter notebooks, LaTeX, and more
    • Page-based: PDF, XPS, CBZ comics
    • Pictures: JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, TIFF, BMP, SVG (all pictures in one job become one gallery)
    • ZIP files: a saved website folder, a folder of pictures (like a comic), or a mix of the files above
    • Not supported: old Office files (.doc, .xls, .ppt — save them in the newer format first), DjVu, CBR/RAR, audio and video
      Very large file? Use its path instead of uploading
